Conviene

Price comparator for supermarkets in Rosario. Search a product once and compare prices and stock across seven online catalogs, with bank promotions from eight chains and a personalized experience.

The idea

Comparing prices means jumping between supermarket sites and bank promotions, with different formats, stock and conditions. Conviene puts that in one place: search a product once and see where it is cheapest.

It is also a portfolio project focused on backend: multi-source search, scraping, caching, authentication and a mobile-first product.

What it does

Multi-store search

Search any product across seven online catalogs at once. Results are on-demand, cached for 10 minutes and include price, stock, image and a link to the store.

Price and stock comparison

Sort by relevance or price, filter by source with one tap, and see partial results when a store fails.

Bank promotions

Promotions from eight chains, filtered by day, chain and payment method, with the full conditions and a link to the source.

Personalization

Create an account to save favorite supermarkets, payment methods and followed products, brands or searches. Promotions start filtered by your preferences.

Mobile-first experience

Search state in the URL, collapsible filters, bottom sheets, keyboard navigation and accessible components.

Full-stack and self-hosted

FastAPI + PostgreSQL/PostGIS + Redis backend, React + TypeScript frontend, daily workers and Docker/Coolify deployment.
